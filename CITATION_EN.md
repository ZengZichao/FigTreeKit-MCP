## What to cite when you use FigTreeKit-MCP

FigTreeKit-MCP delegates tree computation, Nexus generation and rendering to the FigTreeKit kernel. If you publish results produced with this server, please also cite FigTreeKit and the original FigTree software as described in [`NOTICE`](NOTICE). The third-party notices list the relevant projects and licenses.
